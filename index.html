Net4Log Parliament

NET4LOG Parliament

Democracy serves as a fundamental value at the core of Net4Log, guiding its operations and decision-making processes. The Net4Log Parliament is composed of members selected through a democratic election process. Parliamentarians are elected by their peers to serve on specialized committees, each dedicated to key areas aligned with the organization's mission and strategic objectives. This structure ensures full representation and active participation of all members while fostering diverse perspectives for effective decision-making.

Advisory Committee

  • The Advisory Committee consists of four executives.
  • The Advisory Committee provides advice and recommendations to the Parliament on strategic matters.
  • The committee also contributes to business development by leveraging industry networks to drive growth.
  • All policy changes are routed through the Advisory Committee to ensure a democratic process.

Judiciary Committee

  • The Judiciary Committee consists of four executives.
  • The Judiciary Committee ensures compliance with rules and regulations, and resolves disputes within the organisation.
  • Disputes are handled as cases processed by the Net4Log admin team.
  • The Judiciary Committee provides the final adjudication.
CHAIRMAN

The Chairman heads both the Advisory and Judiciary Committees and is elected from among the eight representatives of these committees. The Chairman serves as the final approver of suggestions and decisions proposed by each committee, and ensures the smooth functioning of their activities.

Operation of Parliament

  • 1Any subject to be taken up by the parliamentary committee is proposed in advance by the Net4Log Admin team.
  • 2Based on a suitable date and time, the calendar is booked for an online meeting.
  • 3The Chairman presides over the meeting, seeking votes on the subject before the final decision is made.
  • 4The Net4Log Admin team maintains minutes of the meeting, which are shared with all Trusted Partners.

Freight Protection Plan

NET4LOG Freight
Protection Plan

Net4Log Freight Protection Plan (FPP) is a specialised insurance solution that safeguards Trusted Members against potential financial losses arising from the non-payment of freight invoices due to a partner's bankruptcy or insolvency, ensuring affected members are protected from financial risk.

How does it work?

  • 1Net4Log collects an annual insurance premium from each Trusted Member.
  • 2The amount collected forms the FPP Fund, maintained in a separate account, audited and managed by the FPP Management Team.
  • 3If a Trusted Member becomes insolvent or bankrupt, the affected partner can file a claim with the FPP Management Team.
  • 4The FPP Management Team investigates the claim and determines whether it is covered by the plan.
  • 5The FPP Management Team notifies all network members of the final decision on the claim.
  • 6If the claim is approved, the amount is disbursed from the FPP Fund.

Note: Any claim payment of $1,500 or below will be paid by way of a credit note usable only against future membership fees, to avoid incurring wire fees on small amounts. Total claims are settled on a first-come basis and will not exceed the corpus value of the FPP.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

01What is the Net4Log Freight Protection Plan (FPP)?

An insurance-based financial safeguard that protects Trusted Members from losses due to non-payment of freight invoices if a partner becomes insolvent or bankrupt.

02Who is eligible for the Freight Protection Plan?

The FPP is available to trusted members of the Net4Log network, offering added financial security against partner payment defaults.

03How does the Freight Protection Plan work?

Members contribute an annual premium to the FPP fund. If a partner defaults due to insolvency, the affected member can file a claim; upon approval, compensation is paid from the fund.

04Are there limits on Freight Protection Plan payouts?

Yes, total claim payouts are disbursed on a first-come basis and will not exceed the total corpus value of the FPP fund.
